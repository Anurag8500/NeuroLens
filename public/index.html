Experiment 0: The Leakage Demonstration

Exposing the Danger of Naive Slice Splitting in Medical AI

The 10-20% Metric Inflation Trap

In classical brain MRI benchmarks on Kaggle and published papers, researchers commonly use train_test_split(test_size=0.2) at the random slice level. Because 1 patient contributes 10-25 contiguous 2D slices with identical skull shape, slice splitting causes the network to recognize the patient rather than learn pathology.

CLINICALLY VALID

Patient-Disjoint Partition

  • Zero patient overlap ($P_{train} \cap P_{test} = \emptyset$)
  • Simulates realistic clinical deployment on a new patient
  • Honest Accuracy: 87.50% | Macro-F1: 86.30%
FATALLY FLAWED

Random Slice Split (Leaky)

  • Same patient's slices appear in both train and test
  • Deep network memorizes skull contours & ventricles
  • Falsely inflated expectations that crash upon real-world hospital deployment
